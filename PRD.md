# Nyaya AI

## Vision

Nyaya AI is an AI-powered legal assistant that makes Indian law accessible to everyone in simple language.

Users can ask legal questions, understand their rights, generate legal documents, and receive verified legal information with citations.

---

## Problem

Millions of Indians cannot afford legal consultation and struggle to understand legal procedures.

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Tenants
- Consumers
- Citizens
- Small Businesses

---

## Core Features

### AI Legal Chat

Natural language legal assistant.

---

### RAG

Searches verified Indian legal documents before answering.

---

### Legal Notice Generator

Generate legal notices.

---

### RTI Generator

Generate RTI applications.

---

### Voice Input

Ask questions using voice.

---

### Citation Cards

Every answer includes applicable law and section.
