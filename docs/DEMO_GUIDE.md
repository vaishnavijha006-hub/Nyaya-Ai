# Nyaya AI — Hackathon & Evaluator Demo Guide (5-7 Minute Script)

This guide provides a structured script and operational instructions for presenting **Nyaya AI** to judges, mentors, investors, and legal experts.

---

## Demo Overview & Core Value Proposition

> **The Core Narrative**: Nyaya AI does not merely answer legal questions. It helps citizens move progressively from:  
> **PROBLEM → FACTS → EVIDENCE → LEGAL ANALYSIS → RESOLUTION PATH → ACTION → RESOLUTION**  
> while making pendency-reduction impact visible and avoiding unnecessary court litigation.

## How to Launch Demo Mode

1. Open `http://localhost:3000/demo` in any web browser.
2. Select pre-loaded scenarios:
   - **Scenario A**: Tenant-Landlord Security Deposit Recovery
   - **Scenario B**: Employment Salary & Notice Pay Claim
3. Click through the 7-stage `NyayaPath` interactive stepper to demonstrate intake, facts, evidence audit, 5-level legal analysis, pathway recommendation, action plan, and case package dossier.

---

## Key Legal Safety Principles to Highlight

1. **Informational & Organizational Purpose**: Nyaya AI does not act as a licensed advocate or replace court adjudication.
2. **Zero Win Probability Claims**: Readiness is strictly category-based (No fake "87% win rate").
3. **Privacy Guarantee**: User documents are encrypted and never used for public model training.
4. **Mandatory Advocate Review**: Generated Case Package Dossiers explicitly mandate lawyer review before court reliance.
