=== SLIDE 4 ===
  INNOVATION & DIFFERENTIATION
  What's Genuinely New
  Four modules that don't exist in any current Indian legal-tech app — each one changes the user's pathway, not just their information.
  1
  Two-Sided Pre-Litigation Portal
  Gives the opposite party a real link to respond, builds a confidential settlement dossier, and only escalates to filing if resolution genuinely fails.
  2
  Dispute Readiness & Settlement Score
  Actively changes the user's pathway — file now, fix defects first, or attempt mediation — instead of just handing over information.
  3
  Adjournment & Delay Intelligence
  Quantifies cumulative delay and estimates the cost of the next adjournment — the first litigant-facing delay metric in this space.
  4
  Cluster Case Filing → Systemic Fix
  Detects patterns across many individual complaints (Cluster Case Filing) and converts them into collective filings, PIL outlines, and regulator-facing hotspot dashboards.
  Benchmarked against Nyaya Setu, NyayMitra, AI Legal Guru & Heary-type apps: none actively divert filings, detect clusters, or quantify delay — this is the differentiated core
  4 / 10

=== SLIDE 5 ===
  SOLVING IT: PORTAL & SETTLEMENT SCORE
  Closing the Gaps on Portal & Scoring
  Four open questions across the two-sided settlement portal and the readiness score, and how the design answers them.
  Open Question
  Two-Sided Portal
  What if the opposite party never responds to the pre-litigation notice?
  Can the settlement dossier be used against a user later, discouraging honest talks?
  Settlement Score & Liability
  Could a strong case get mis-scored as "mediate" and cost the user their rights?
  What is Nyaya AI's liability if a completeness check misses something?
  Our Fix
  Two-Sided Portal
  No response → auto-escalates to Lok Adalat/court; logged as neutral, never as fault. All talks stay confidential "without prejudice" under the Mediation Act, 2023.
  Settlement Score & Liability
  Score is advisory only — a hard-coded limitation-period check overrides it near deadlines; every recommendation is flagged for advocate review before use, mirroring NALSA’s Tele-Law disclaimer model.
  Source: Mediation Act, 2023; Limitation Act, 1963; NALSA Tele-Law disclaimer model
  5 / 10

=== SLIDE 6 ===
  SOLVING IT: DELAY, CLUSTERS & LEGAL AID
  Delay Data, Clustering & Aid Eligibility
  Open questions across delay tracking, cluster/PIL detection, and legal-aid eligibility — and how the design answers them.
  Open Question
  Delay Intelligence
  Is adjournment/delay data manually entered, or pulled from a real source?
  Cluster Case Filing & PIL
  How is a false cluster match prevented, e.g. two tenants sharing a landlord?
  Once PIL-flagged, who actually takes the matter forward?
  Legal Aid Eligibility
  Section 12 LSA has overlapping grounds — does the user reach the right DLSA office?
  Our Fix
  Delay Intelligence
  Two-tier pipeline: NJDG/eCourts data for digitised courts, flagged "user-reported" fallback elsewhere — calibrated against real disposal rates (1.8Cr disposed vs 2Cr filed, 2023).
  Cluster Case Filing & PIL
  Multi-factor match (entity + incident window + issue category) with mandatory human confirmation — no auto-enrollment; PIL matters route to empanelled advocates via DLSA/NALSA, never self-filed.
  Legal Aid Eligibility
  Eligibility coded deterministically from Section 12 & NALSA guidelines; user is matched to nearest DLSA with documents needed; borderline cases go to manual review.
  Source: NJDG/eCourts; Constitution Art. 32 & 226, PIL Rules 2013; Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987
  6 / 10

=== SLIDE 8 ===
  SCALABILITY & BUSINESS MODEL
  Built to Scale — and Pay Off
  1
  Modular engines
  Triage, cluster, settlement, ADR and delay-tracking each run as independent services — they can be scaled or deployed per state/DLSA without touching the others.
  2
  Multi-tenant by design
  Session isolation (journey_state.py) already separates users/tenants, so onboarding new states or institutional partners doesn't require re-architecture.
  3
  Vector search at volume
  Cluster detection runs on Qdrant vector similarity, built for semantic matching across millions of complaints, not a handful.
  4
  B2C — Citizens
  Free tier for queries & eligibility check; Pro Citizen ₹199–499/mo unlocks triage, portal & delay tracker; pay-per-document ₹99–299.
  5
  B2B — Advocates & DLSAs
  Advocate Suite ₹1,499–2,999/mo for case-package intake & cluster leads; hotspot/pattern dashboards licensed to DLSAs and regulators.
  6
  Traction Proof
  NALSA's 1st National Lok Adalat 2026 disposed 2.84 Cr cases worth ₹10,920+ Cr in a single day — citizens & institutions already engage once pre-litigation settlement is made easy.
  Pricing indicative. Traction: NALSA, 1st National Lok Adalat 2026 (14 March 2026) disposal figures
